# ecs_170_project
Our primary project for ECS 170: Intro to Machine Learning

During an Artificial Intelligence Course, my group and I were tasked with making a project that involved AI in some capacity. We chose to create a replication of the popular problem in ethics known as the Trolley Problem. Each scenario presented keywords with weights based on the ethical principle they aligned with (Utilitarianism, Deontology, Virtue Ethics, etc.). In our version, instead of a human player who chose an answer to a scenario, we also asked three different AI models: ChatGPT, Claude, and Grok. In the end, after the models have given a decisions to all the scenarios, their choices were then compared to human choices.
